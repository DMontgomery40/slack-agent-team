Use the selected channel/thread style. Keep meaningful decisions, accountable owners, and blockers visible to the appropriate audience. Link detailed artifacts. Routine unchanged status stays quiet. A connection receipt is not task completion. Participants should be able to explain their identity, current session, available capabilities, and allowed scope from the verified roster.
